Building a Smart ESP32 Sensor

Posted on June 28, 2026 · by SKR Electronics Lab

In this tutorial, we'll build a wireless temperature and humidity sensor using the ESP32-S3. The firmware connects to Wi-Fi and publishes data via MQTT every 30 seconds.

You'll need an ESP32-S3 or ESP32 development board and a USB cable. No IDE or drivers required — everything happens in your browser.

⚡ Install Firmware

Connect your ESP board via USB and click the button below. The installer will auto-detect your chip and flash the correct firmware.

Wiring Guide

Connect the DHT22 sensor to GPIO4 (data), VCC to 3.3V, and GND to GND. The onboard LED on GPIO2 will blink once per second after a successful connection.

Once flashed, open the Serial Monitor at 115200 baud to see the device logs, or watch the LED pattern — 3 quick blinks means Wi-Fi connected.
